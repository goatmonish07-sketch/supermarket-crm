# Products & Inventory — overrides

- List density: medium; desktop table, mobile cards with image/initial tile, name, variant count, price range, stock pill.
- Filters as horizontally-wrapping pill chips (type, stock status) + category select + search; filters live in the URL (deep-linkable, back-safe).
- Editor: single page, sectioned cards (Basics → Pricing & tax → Variants → Stock). Sticky bottom action bar on mobile with Save.
- Variant generator: chip pickers for sizes and colours (presets + custom), live matrix preview, per-row overrides; never more than 100 variants per product.
- Services hide stock and variants; show "Price at counter" toggle and turnaround days.
- Labels page is print-first: on-screen controls hidden by `@media print`.
