# Orders & jobs — overrides

- Two tabs: **Orders** and **Alterations & stitching**; filters (Due today, Overdue, Mine, On hold) live in the URL.
- **Board ≥ 1024px:** one column per stage; the board scrolls horizontally inside its own container (never the page). Cards have a visible "Next: <stage>" button — no drag-and-drop required (keyboard/touch friendly).
- **< 1024px:** stage chips + a single list; same cards.
- Cards: number (mono), customer, due date (overdue = danger colour + icon + text "Overdue"), assignee avatar, balance, hold badge.
- Detail: stage stepper at top (current stage emphasised, completed stages ticked), primary action = next stage or "Create bill" when Ready.
- Job slip prints two copies (customer + workshop) with a CODE128 barcode of the job number.
